How to read this. Each tile shows the latest public price (large) with its short-term move, and below it the 2026 year-to-date average price plus how far the current price sits above or below that average (% vs avg) — so you can see at a glance whether today's level is rich or cheap versus the year's running average. The colored spine and flag mark data quality: Live figures auto-refresh each morning; Proxy figures stand in for benchmarks behind paid services (Argus, ICIS, Fastmarkets, Fertecon) and are directional.

What updates automatically. The Live tiles — natural gas, COMEX copper, the grains & cattle, Class III milk, the FX pairs, and the Urea & DAP FOB CME swaps — are pulled fresh every morning by an automated job (Yahoo Finance for the futures & FX, farmbucks for the two fertilizer swaps), with no one having to touch it. The Lagged and Proxy tiles — MAP, potash & UAN FOB and the retail fertilizer averages (no exchange contract exists for MAP or potash, and UAN barely trades), LME metals, sulfuric acid, manganese, freight indices and the Panama draft — have no free daily feed, so they carry the most recent published print and are topped up periodically. Their as-of date is shown on each tile.

On the AVG YTD figure. The average is the 2026 year-to-date average price. It is measured where a free daily/monthly 2026 history exists (FX, natural gas, milk, metals) and estimated where the full history is paywalled (freight, grains, cattle, Gulf/NOLA fertilizer) — treat the % vs avg there as directional. UAN shows no average (barely-traded contract).
